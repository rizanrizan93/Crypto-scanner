Frozen before simulation.
Entry: exact next H4 open after a completed signal candle.
LONG stop=entry-1.5*signal_ATR14; target=entry+3.0*signal_ATR14.
SHORT stop=entry+1.5*signal_ATR14; target=entry-3.0*signal_ATR14.
Max hold=12 H4 bars; otherwise exit at close of 12th held bar.
If stop and target both touch in one H4 candle, STOP_FIRST.
Funding timestamps counted only when entry_time < funding_time < exit_event_time; stop/target exit_event_time is the end of the triggering H4 candle.
1R is absolute entry-to-stop distance; costs and funding are converted to R by dividing notional return by stop-distance return.
Bootstrap: 200 circular block trials over closed-trade net R, block length 20 trades.
